Privacy PolicySupport

Plant Discover has no account, ads or analytics. Photos you choose, observations and notes are stored on your device. Recognition runs on the device. On Android, Google Play may download the model and content before offline use.

Three days of full access are included. An optional one-time purchase then keeps access; nothing renews automatically. Apple or Google handles payment and purchase verification. The app keeps access records locally, including secure storage.

You can delete saved observations in the app. Removing the app removes its local files; system backups, shared copies and secure purchase records may remain. Manage those through your device, store or recipient. For help or privacy questions, email rizkcorsight@rizkcorsight.com.
